## How I lead

- **Outcomes before output.** Roadmaps and engineering metrics should connect directly to customer value and business performance.
- **Reliability is a product capability.** Quality, security, observability, accessibility, and recovery belong inside delivery—not after it.
- **AI needs operating boundaries.** Useful automation combines human review, provenance, measurable acceptance criteria, and explicit authority.
- **Architecture and organization design are connected.** Clear domain ownership and decision rights make both systems and teams easier to evolve.
- **Visibility creates alignment.** Strong operating cadences give teams autonomy while giving executives an honest view of delivery, risk, and tradeoffs.
